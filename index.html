Indiclock — molecular layer
Frozen v2.1: GSE40279 + GSE87571 whole-blood 450K data, 275 CpG features, Elastic Net alpha 0.5. External GSE55763 Indian-classified subset: R=0.941, MAE=3.50 years, RMSE=4.37 years, without retraining.
IndiBio — physiological layer
38 biomarkers across seven physiological domains. Internal held-out R=0.893, MAE=6.47 years. NHANES 2003–04 temporal validation: R=0.769, MAE=8.79 years.
Indian transportability programme
NidaanKosha contains ~6.8 million laboratory records/readings across ~100,000 document IDs. These are treated as report-level evidence, not 100,000 unique people. No frozen IndiBio coefficient has been changed on this evidence alone.
Paired multi-omics mapping
Next bridge: molecular age ↔ physiological age, CpG ↔ physiological domains and, where sample size permits, CpG ↔ individual biomarkers. The question is whether the layers overlap, complement one another or diverge.
