IndiBio — physiological layer

Blood chemistry and hematology are comparatively accessible and already embedded in clinical workflows. IndiBio is designed to interpret them as a coordinated physiological ageing system rather than as disconnected lab values.

Indiclock — molecular layer

The frozen v2.1 model uses 275 CpG features selected from a larger age-associated feature space. These features are predictive model inputs; they are not individually claimed to be causal aging markers.

Mapping layer

In paired participants, the programme will test molecular age ↔ physiological age, CpG ↔ physiological-domain relationships and, where sample size permits, CpG ↔ individual biomarker associations.

Future Biological State Engine

Inputs: molecular state + physiological state + age/sex/context + longitudinal change.

Output: an interpretable profile of trajectories, discordance and biological state that can eventually support personalized intervention and re-audit.

The integrated state engine is a product direction and active research programme, not yet a clinically validated product.

Population-aware biology

Why India first?

Population-aware measurement is not an assumption that one Indian profile represents everyone. It is a scientific decision to test transportability where biological reference distributions, cardiometabolic risk and healthcare context may differ.

Different reference context

Research in Asian Indian and broader South Asian populations has described higher body-fat and central/visceral-fat burden at lower BMI levels than in many white European populations.

Different risk context

South Asian populations have been reported to show cardiometabolic risk at comparatively lower levels of adiposity. This motivates testing whether biological reference models transfer unchanged.

Large real-world opportunity

India combines population scale, diverse environments and a major preventive-health need — creating a meaningful setting in which to test population-aware biological intelligence.

Population specificity is tested, not assumed

Aeterna evaluates population specificity in sequence: reference distributions → age trajectories → eventually model effects. Only the last level could justify changing a frozen model coefficient.

These population differences motivate transportability testing; they do not establish that all Indians or South Asians share one distinct biological phenotype.

Evidence first. Claims second.

The fastest way to understand Aeterna today is through four numbers — followed by the scientific caveats behind them.

0.941Pearson R

What this demonstrates

The frozen 275-CpG model transferred strongly to the Indian-classified external subset without retraining or recalibration.

What it does not demonstrate

It does not prove that Indiclock has already been optimized specifically for Indian biology, clinically validated, or causally linked to disease or lifespan.

IndiBio: the next layer

Frozen NHANES-derived IndiBio has 38 biomarkers across seven domains. Temporal validation in NHANES 2003–04 produced Pearson R 0.769 and MAE 8.79 years, making transportability and calibration central development questions.

Indian transportability programme

NidaanKosha
Large-scale Indian laboratory/report-level evidence

~6.8 million laboratory records/readings across ~100,000 document IDs. These documents are not treated as 100,000 unique people.

Findings
Biomarker-specific transportability signals

Distributional and age-trajectory signals have been identified across metabolic, cardiovascular, renal, hematologic/iron and hepatic/protein domains.

Scientific filter
Replication before recalibration

HOMA-IR and transferrin-receptor incompatibilities are separated from biological signals. ALT/AST trajectory signals require independent Indian participant-level replication; KN13 shows they are not automatically India-specific.

Next

The current programme demonstrates transportability signals and identifies calibration questions. It does not claim that IndiBio is already Indian-validated or that frozen coefficients have been changed.
