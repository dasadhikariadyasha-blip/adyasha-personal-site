The system

Five layers. One longitudinal loop.

Aeterna does not begin with one universal health score. It begins with independently measurable layers, connects them, and follows how the state changes over time.

01
Physiological layer · today

IndiBio

What is it? A routine-blood-biomarker framework organized into seven physiological systems: metabolic, inflammatory, cardiovascular, renal, hematologic/iron, hepatic/protein and nutritional.

What does it state? The physiological state of the body across multiple systems — and, ultimately, how that state changes with age.

02
Molecular layer · today

Indiclock

What is it? A frozen 275-CpG DNA-methylation biological-age model.

What does it state? A molecular-aging estimate derived from epigenetic patterns in blood.

Current external Indian-classified validation: R 0.941 · MAE 3.50 years · RMSE 4.37 years. Frozen model; no retraining for this validation.

The thesis

Population matters.

A model can perform well in one population and still need careful transportability testing elsewhere. Biology, baseline distributions and relationships between risk factors are not guaranteed to transfer unchanged across populations.

Why population-specific?
Same number. Different biological meaning.

Body composition, biomarker distributions, disease-risk relationships and age trajectories can differ across populations. Population-aware modelling asks whether a signal behaves the same way before treating it as universal.

What Aeterna does differently
Validate transferability before recalibrating.

Aeterna separates baseline transportability, age-trajectory transportability and model-effect transportability. A population-specific coefficient is a conclusion to earn, not an assumption to make.

Why India → South Asia

A different biological context deserves to be measured.

Research across Asian and South Asian populations describes higher visceral and ectopic adiposity, altered insulin biology and cardiometabolic risk at lower BMI thresholds than in many Western populations. These are population-level patterns, not a claim that every individual is biologically the same.

01 · Body composition

Adiposity is not just BMI.

Asian populations can carry greater visceral and ectopic fat at lower BMI, making BMI alone an incomplete representation of metabolic state.

02 · Metabolic physiology

Risk can emerge earlier.

Reviews of Indian populations describe distinctive insulin secretion/resistance phenotypes and younger diabetes presentation in some groups.

03 · Health burden

Prevention has a narrow window.

WHO reports a major NCD burden across South-East Asia, with a substantial proportion of NCD deaths occurring prematurely.

Evidence: Nature Reviews Endocrinology (2026) · Nature Reviews Endocrinology (2022) · WHO South-East Asia
